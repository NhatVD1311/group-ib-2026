Executive Finding: The Balonx Sistema has expanded its operational capabilities beyond traditional Phishing-as-a-Service (PhaaS) models, such as web-based phishing and malicious APKs. It now includes a parallel, automated vishing service utilizing generative AI technologies, including synthetic voice generation, conversational Large Language Models (LLMs), and Whisper-based Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR). This module serves as an independent product offering for affiliates, coexisting with existing infrastructure. Access to the Administration Panel As of the time of drafting this report, investigators have identified a single domain associated with this nascent fraudulent operation: callbalonx[.]info . The authentication interface for this domain is illustrated in 2. Utilizing the default credentials discovered during the technical analysis, the access was successfully established to the administrative management panel. Figure 5 . Landing panel of CallFlow. The landing dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of operational data, including active calls, daily call volume, success rates, and calls currently in queue. Furthermore, the interface displays active campaigns, the total number of phone records within the associated database, and the average duration of telephonic engagements. Technical examination of additional repositories associated with the identified GitHub account revealed a dataset comprising telephone numbers specifically related to BANAMEX. Preliminary analysis suggests this database serves as a primary data source for the automated Scam system and the Phishing system. Figure 6. Possible database numbers. Through a comprehensive examination of all panel sections, several names and financial institutions targeted by this campaign were successfully identified. Figure 7. Managers section of the panel. The administrative interface contains usernames and associated Telegram contact numbers for the operators. Furthermore, a specific campaign targeting Banorte was identified. Within the "Flujos" (Flows) section, additional fraudulent campaigns affecting Coppel, BANAMEX, and INVEX were also observed. Conclusion In conclusion, while the analyzed platform is in an early stage of development, it represents a threat with the potential for significant impact. The evolving threat landscape has transcended traditional server attack vectors, migrating toward automated fraud models that integrate generative artificial intelligence to scale their operational capabilities and increase their productive efficiency. However, this reliance on automation in tool development carries an inherent vulnerability. These flaws in project construction, or technological "crumbs", leave critical traces that act as strategic advantages for our teams, allowing us to identify security gaps, as demonstrated by the detected credential leak, and strengthening our response and mitigation capabilities against these types of emerging threats.
